# AI Assistant

NetFlow includes a powerful, context-aware AI Assistant designed to help users quickly find information, track requests, and build workflows effortlessly. 

## Global Assistant Widget
The floating AI Assistant is available across the platform. You can ask it questions in natural language, such as:
- *"Where is my leave request?"*
- *"Who is pending approval for the IT equipment form?"*
- *"Show me all rejected requests from this week."*

The assistant uses real-time data to provide accurate, actionable answers without requiring you to navigate through multiple menus.

## AI Form & Workflow Builder
For Workspace Builders and Admins, the AI Assistant accelerates the creation process.
Instead of manually dragging and dropping fields, you can simply describe what you need:
> *"Create a travel reimbursement form with an attachment field for receipts, and route it to the direct manager for approval."*

The AI will automatically generate the form fields and configure the appropriate workflow routing based on your prompt.

## Prerequisites
> [!NOTE]
> The AI features are only visible if the system administrator has configured a valid LLM provider in the platform environment variables. If it is not configured, the floating widget and AI generation tools will be hidden.
