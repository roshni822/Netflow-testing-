# Analytics

Analytics summarizes forms, workflows, and tasks so builders can see volume, throughput, and bottlenecks.

::: tip Who can view
Usually available to report roles such as Admin, CEO, Manager, HR, VP. If the sidebar has no **Analytics** item, your role does not include reports.
:::

## Step 1 — Open Analytics

1. In the left sidebar, under **REPORTS**, click **Analytics**.

   ![Analytics overview](/screenshots/guide/analytics-overview.png)

2. Wait for charts and summary cards to load.

**You should see:** the **Analytics & reports** page with metrics for your organization only.

## Step 2 — Read the overview

1. Check high-level counts (executions, completions, pending work — labels match your build).
2. Scan charts for trends over the selected period.
3. Note departments or workflows that look slow or overloaded.

## Step 3 — Filter the view

1. Change the **date range** (for example last 7 / 30 / 90 days).
2. Narrow by **department** using the picker next to it.
3. Re-read the charts after each filter change.

**You should see:** numbers update to match the filter — useful for monthly reviews.

## Whose numbers are these?

The line under the page title says what the figures cover, because it is not the
same for everyone:

| You are | The page reports on |
| --- | --- |
| Admin or CEO | The whole workspace |
| Manager, HR, VP | Your team — the people who report to you |

A leader's department picker starts at **My team**; choosing a department narrows
within that and can never show you a team you do not lead. Forms and workflow
counts stay workspace-wide, since those are a shared catalogue rather than
anyone's own work.

## Step 4 — Export (if available)

1. Look for **Export** / download actions on the page header.
2. Download CSV or the offered format for offline reporting.

## What success looks like

You can answer: “How many requests this month?”, “Where do we bottleneck?”, and “Is SLA healthy?” without opening every task.
