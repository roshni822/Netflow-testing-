# Notifications

Notifications tell you about new assignments, decisions, and escalations. You can skim them from the bell or open the full list.

## Step 1 — Check the bell

1. Look at the **bell** icon in the top bar.
2. If a badge shows a number, you have unread items.
3. Click the bell to open the dropdown.

   ![Notification bell](/screenshots/guide/notifications-bell.png)

4. Click an item to open the related task or page (when linked).

**You should see:** recent alerts without leaving your current screen.

## Step 2 — Open the full Notifications page

1. Open **Notifications** (from the bell “view all” link, or navigate to `/notifications`).

   ![Notifications list](/screenshots/guide/notifications-list.png)

2. Scan the list newest-first.
3. Mark items read as you open them, or clear/delete when your UI offers it.

**You should see:** “You’re all caught up” when the list is empty.

## What typically creates a notification

| Event | Who gets notified |
| --- | --- |
| Task assigned to you | Assignee |
| Approval / rejection on your request | Submitter |
| SLA escalation | Escalation target (and sometimes admins) |
| Workflow **Notify** node | Configured recipients (in-app in the current build) |

::: tip Workflow Notify node
Designers can add a **Notify** step on the canvas. See [Workflows → Node types](/guide/workflows).
:::

## What success looks like

You never miss a pending approval: bell badge → open task → act in [Tasks & approvals](/guide/tasks-approvals).
